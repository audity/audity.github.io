Ingredients:
- 1 onion, sliced
- rice
- 1 lb thinly sliced beef
- 1/2 cup corn kernels
- 1 tbspn butter
- 2 green onions, chopped
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

Sauce:
- 3 tbspn soy sauce
- 3 tbspn oyster sauce
- 1 tbspn honey
- 2 tbspn mirin
- 3 garlic cloves

Instructions
1. Make rice
2. Combine sauce
3. Saute onions for 4 minutes in butter
4. Saute your beef with the onions
5. Add corn and green onions and sprinkle with pepper
6. Drizzle sauce and mix!
