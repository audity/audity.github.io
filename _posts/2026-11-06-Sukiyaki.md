Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup mirin
- 1/2 cup sake
- 2 tbspn sugar
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1 large green onion
- dried shiitake mushrooms
- 1 pack firm tofu
- 1 lb thinly sliced ribeye (or any meat)

Instructions:
1. Soak the dried shiitake mushrooms in some water (I microwave them in water to have them hydrate faster)
2. Wash all of your ingredients
3. In a pot, heat mirin, sake and sugar on medium heat until the sugar is dissolved an alcohol evaporated (3-5 min)
4. In a pot, heat some oil and char the green onions
5. Add the rest of the ingredients
6. Pour the sauce and the water from the shiitake mushrooms and simmer until everything is cooked
7. Optional (dip everything in a sauce of one beaten egg)
