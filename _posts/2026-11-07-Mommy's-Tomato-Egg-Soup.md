Ingredients:
- 3 tomatos
- 1 tbspn oil
- 3 cups water
- 1 tbspn dried shrimp
- 1 tbspn picked vegetable (pickled mustard tuber)
- 1 egg
- 1 stalk green onion
- opt. 3 stalks cilantro
- salt to taste
- sesame oil
- 1 tbspn dried seaweed

Instructions
1. Dice tomatoes into small peices
2. Put oil into a pot and stir fry the tomatoes until the juice comes out
3. Add in water and brting to a boil
4. Soak the shrimp and add the shrimp into the water
5. Beat eggs and pour the eggs into the rolling boil, let it sit for a few seconds before stirring
6. Add pickled vegtables, diced green onion and seaweed
7. Salt and sesame oil to taste
